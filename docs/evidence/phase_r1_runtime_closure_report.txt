--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Buffer Operation: WheelOdometryBuffer receives /diff_drive_controller/odom, maintains a 5-second sliding window, handles out-of-order timestamps via sorted insertion, and computes geodesic SE(3) delta transform (delta = T_begin^-1 * T_end).
- Scan Registration Coupling:
  * In LidarOdometryServer.cpp, the computed wheel prior delta is passed directly to KinematicICP::RegisterFrame(points, timestamps, extrinsic, delta).
  * When wheel prior is missing, scan registration is safely deferred.
  * When wheel prior delta is nonzero, it acts as the initial guess and optimization regularization constraint.
